Methodology & assumptions

Processing approach

  1. Build a regular 5 km navigation grid across the Gulf of Finland study bounds.
  2. Mask land and shallow cells using coastline, island, and bathymetry layers.
  3. Align hourly wind, wave, current, and ice fields to every navigable cell.
  4. Compute seasonal mean, maximum, P50, P90, and P99 statistics.
  5. Map AIS tracks to cells and use medoid sequences as regular routes.
  6. Apply vessel-specific caution and danger thresholds to route costs.

Important limitations

  • Displayed conditions are illustrative until live feeds are connected.
  • Vessel particulars are engineering proxies, not vessel certificates.
  • Risk bands are planning assumptions, not legal operating limits.
  • Digitraffic AIS excludes fishing vessels; HELCOM density is the fallback.
  • Operational navigation requires official charts and master judgement.
